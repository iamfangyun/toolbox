Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 13, 2026

Overview

ToolBox (tools.bmaster.cn) is a purely front-end online tools website. We take your privacy very seriously. This privacy policy explains how we handle your information.

Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data.

  • All tool calculations are performed locally in your browser
  • Text and data you enter is never sent to any server
  • We do not require account registration
  • We do not collect names, emails, phone numbers, or other personal information

Cookies and Local Storage

We use your browser's local storage (localStorage) to save your preferences:

  • Language preference (Chinese/English)

This data is stored only on your device and is never sent to us. You can delete this information at any time by clearing your browser data.

Third-Party Services

Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google may use cookies to serve ads based on your interests. You can manage your ad preferences at Google Ads Settings.

Cookies used by Google include:

  • __gads — Ad personalization
  • __gac — Ad conversion tracking
  • IDE — Ad performance measurement

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

Google Analytics

We may use Google Analytics to understand website traffic (visits, page views, etc.). Information collected by Google Analytics is anonymous and does not include personal identification.

Third-Party Links

This website may contain links to other websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites and recommend reading their privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

This website is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

DMCA / Copyright

If you believe any content on this website infringes your copyright, please contact us and we will address it promptly.

Policy Updates
